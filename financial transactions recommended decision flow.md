Journal General Debit or Credit Adjustments should only be used for accounting corrections where no invoice correction process is appropriate. In most member-related scenarios, Credit Notes, Fee Adjustments, Fee Increase/Decrease processes, or Write-Offs should be used instead, as these preserve the correct membership and invoice audit trail. Journal adjustments are typically required for back-office financial corrections such as correcting ledger postings, deferred income adjustments, opening balance corrections, rounding discrepancies, migration issues, or reallocating balances between accounting periods where no member-facing invoice change is required. Journal adjustments should not normally be used to reverse invoices, refund members, correct membership renewals, or process category changes, as these should be handled through operational finance processes such as Credit Notes, Refunds, Fee Adjustments, or Write-Offs.
